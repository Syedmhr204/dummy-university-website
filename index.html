A modern university with deep roots

University of MHR was founded to make quality higher education reachable for students across Bangladesh. We pair rigorous academics with industry-linked projects, research labs and a campus culture built on respect and curiosity.

From engineering and business to health sciences and law, every program is taught by qualified faculty led by our Chairman and Heads of Department.
