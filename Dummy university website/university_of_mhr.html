ভর্তি প্রক্রিয়া (Admissions Process)

Your journey starts here. Applications for Fall 2026 are now open.

1

অনলাইন আবেদন (Apply Online)

Submit through our Online Admission Portal or collect form from campus. Fee: ৳ 1,000 (non-refundable).

2

ডকুমেন্টস জমা (Submit Documents)

SSC & HSC transcripts, Testimonial, Character Certificate, 4 passport photos, NID/Birth Certificate, and Quota documents if applicable.

3

ভর্তি পরীক্ষা (Admission Test)

Written test for Engineering & Medical. Viva voce for Business & Arts. Test dates published on website.

4

ফলাফল ও ভর্তি (Result & Enrollment)

Merit list published online. Selected candidates must pay ৳ 25,000 admission fee within 7 days to confirm seat.

📋 Application Requirements by Program

Undergraduate (স্নাতক)

  • SSC & HSC / Equivalent passed
  • Minimum GPA: 2.50 (combined)
  • Engineering: Math & Physics required
  • Medical: Biology & Chemistry required
  • Valid HSC/equivalent certificate
  • NID or Birth Certificate

Graduate (স্নাতকোত্তর)

  • 4-year Bachelor's degree
  • Minimum CGPA: 2.50 / 2nd Class
  • MBA: Any discipline accepted
  • M.Sc.: Relevant Bachelor's required
  • Work experience preferred (EMBA)
  • Recommendation letters (2)
